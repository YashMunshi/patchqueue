# PatchQueue — visual identity

Industrial work-order desk.

- Display: DejaVu Sans Condensed; text: IBM Plex Sans.
- Palette: #eae7e0 ground, #2a2a24 text, #a44420 primary accent.
- Header: custom queue symbol and individual wordmark.
- Composition: queue concept diagram, project-specific controls and results.
- Type: 18px body, 16px support, 15px labels; fluid display sizes.
- Hairline borders, 3px control radius, 160ms interactions, reduced motion.
- Font licensing and notices: web/fonts/Brand-Licenses.txt.

The header illustrations explain the model; they are not operational metrics.
